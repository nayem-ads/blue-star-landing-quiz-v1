How do I know you’re legitimate?

Check our license, 2B60428, on the California Department of Insurance website. Our fee and your right to cancel are written into the contract before any work starts.

Will this slow my claim down?

We have a strong financial incentive to expedite settlement on your behalf.

Will my insurer hold it against me?

Carriers work with public adjusters every day. The difference is what gets measured.

What does it cost?

No upfront cost. We’re paid a percentage of what we recover, agreed in writing first.

I already cashed a check. Is it too late?

Usually not. In California our fee can never leave you with less than the insurer paid before you hired us.

Do I still pick my own contractor?

Yes. We don’t arrange repairs. We price the work your contractor will do.

My adjuster keeps changing.

We keep the full file, every letter and every estimate, so a new adjuster on their side starts from our record, not from zero.
